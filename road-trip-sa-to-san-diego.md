# San Antonio → San Diego: cheapest safe CCS-only charging plan
**Car:** 2023 Hyundai Ioniq 5 Long Range (77.4 kWh, 74 kWh usable), CCS1, no NACS adapter, so every Tesla site is excluded.
**Route:** I-10 W → I-8 W at Casa Grande. About **1,262 mi** from the IONNA at 5602 UTSA Blvd to downtown San Diego.

## How much longer?
- **Total time:** about **22½ hours**.
  - About 18 h of driving.
  - About 3 h 47 min of charging across 12 stops.
  - About 1 h of plugging in and paying.
- **Split it into two days with a night in Las Cruces:**
  - **Day 1:** about 10½ h. Leave 7:00 CDT, reach Las Cruces about 16:40 MDT, including the Las Cruces charge.
  - **Day 2:** about 12 h. Leave 7:00 MDT, reach San Diego about 18:00 PDT.
  - Westbound you gain 1 hour on each day's clock.
- **If you're still on the $0.20 IONNA charger now:** 80→100% takes about 20 minutes, and it is the cheapest energy of the trip (about $3 for the last 15 kWh). That fill is not counted in the totals below.

## Totals

- **Total cost at non-member prices:** about **$249**.
- **Charging time:** about **3 h 47 min**, plus about 1 h for plugging in and paying at 12 stops.
- **Driving:** about 18 h.
- **Baselines (same energy):**
  - "Just use Electrify America everywhere" at guest prices costs about **$283**. That uses EA at Junction, Ozona, Fort Stockton, Van Horn, El Paso, Deming, Lordsburg, Benson, Marana, Dateland, Yuma and El Centro. This plan saves about **$33**.

## Membership advice
- **Buy Electrify America Pass+ for one month: $7, about 25% off. Cancel after the trip.**
  - The plan buys about 185 kWh at EA anyway: Ozona, Fort Stockton, Van Horn, El Paso, Marana and Dateland. Pass+ pays for itself in less than one stop.
  - Hyundai's free EA sessions for 2023 buyers lasted 2 years, so they have most likely expired.
- **With Pass+, make these swaps:**
  - **Yuma:** charge at **EA Target** ($0.45) instead of Pilot ($0.54). Keep Pilot as the backup.
  - **El Centro (optional):** EA Main St at $0.45 if it is working, which saves about $2 more. Keep EV Range as the backup.
- **Keep Kerrville** ($0.30), **Las Cruces** ($0.44), **Lordsburg Flying J** and **Willcox IONNA** ($0.39). They are still cheaper than EA, and the EA sites at Lordsburg and Deming are unreliable.
- **Skip the others.** Tesla's membership is no use without an adapter. EVgo Plus/PlusMax don't pay off here: the only EVgo-branded site, Alpine, is a backup, and it's unknown whether EVgo plans apply at Pilot/Flying J eXtend. IONNA and ChargePoint have no membership.

## Overnight
- **Recommended: Las Cruces** (mile 580).
  - It splits the trip into about 10½ h and 12 h days.
  - It is where the cheap $0.44 DC charger is.
  - Do the 47-minute Doña Ana charge on arrival, or the next morning in daylight.
- **Hotel L2 saves the most.** Book a Las Cruces hotel with free J1772 charging (use PlugShare's lodging filter; no specific hotel was verified).
  - Charge El Paso only to **50%**. You arrive at about 23%.
  - Skip the Doña Ana DC session and plug in overnight. The car's 10.9 kW onboard charger fills 23→100% in about 6 h on an 11 kW unit, or about 9 h on 7 kW.
  - That saves about **$31 at non-member prices (about $28 with Pass+)** and 47 minutes.
  - If the hotel charger is taken, Doña Ana's DC charger is in the same town and open 24 h.
- **El Paso** also works, but Day 2 grows to about 13 h.
- **Tucson is not recommended.** Day 1 would run about 14–15 h.

## Warnings
- **No CCS backups in West Texas.** Ozona, Fort Stockton and Van Horn are each a single 4-stall EA site (2019–2020 hardware), and the Tesla sites are excluded. Check EA app stall status before every leg. If a site is down, wait for it, use hotel L2, or drive 60–65 mph from 100%. Never set off hoping.
- **Longest legs:**
  - Marana → Dateland, **153 mi**. Leave Marana at 100%; Gila Bend Rivian is the bail-out at the 101-mile point.
  - Kerrville → Ozona, **144 mi**. Leave Kerrville at 100%; Junction is the bail-out at the 51-mile point.
  - Fort Stockton → Van Horn, 118 mi with about 1,000 ft of climb.
  - Las Cruces → Lordsburg, 118 mi.
  - El Centro → San Diego, 119 mi over the mountains.
  - Van Horn → El Paso, 112 mi.
- **Headwind.** Westbound trips often face a W or SW wind. With 15 mph or more, the range model allows only about 130 mi from 100% and 100 mi from 80%. Use the bail-outs above, or drop to 70 mph, which recovers about 15%.
- **Reliability problems:**
  - EA Deming, EA Lordsburg Maverik and EA El Centro have user reports of failures (backups only).
  - Viejas Casino in Alpine scores 4.1/10 and has only 2 CCS working, not the 16 announced. Avoid it.
  - Doña Ana ChargePoint has 1 of 2 ports reported offline.
  - EA El Paso Walmart may have only one 350 kW unit.
- **Price conflicts and fees:**
  - Lordsburg Flying J: $0.53 vs. $0.69.
  - Fort Stockton: $0.60–0.72.
  - EA idle fee: $0.40/min after 10 min. Rivian Gila Bend: $0.50/min idle fee and the Rivian app is required.
- **Stations to avoid:**
  - **NACS-only:** Rivian Jacumba (the only charger on the I-8 grade) and every Tesla site.
  - **Could not be re-found:** "ChargePoint NLR ELP C#1" and Bravo Cadillac in El Paso.
  - **Mislabeled data:** Shell Recharge Junction.
  - No station in the verified data was flagged "temporarily unavailable". Re-check PlugShare or dcfctracker the morning of each day.
- **California prices jump** after El Centro: EVgo Alpine $0.69, EA California average $0.69. That's why the plan buys in Yuma and only tops up in El Centro.
- **The I-8 climb:**
  - In-Ko-Pah grade: about 3,000 ft in 11 mi. Crestwood Summit, at 4,190 ft, is the highest point on I-8.
  - The climb takes about 8.5–9.5 kWh, about 12% of the pack.
  - Leave El Centro at 80% or more. You should crest at about 40% and arrive in San Diego at about 22%.
  - There is no usable CCS charger between El Centro and Alpine (about 84 mi).
- **Apps to install:** Electrify America (with Pass+), ChargePoint, Pilot Flying J/EVgo, bp pulse, and Rivian (backup only). IONNA takes tap-to-pay cards.

## Standard Range (58 kWh, 54 kWh usable)
The Standard Range's safe leg is only about 105 mi from 100% (about 85 mi from 80%) at 75–80 mph. Changes it needs:
- **Junction becomes a required stop** (Pilot, to 100%). Kerrville → Ozona at 144 mi is impossible; Kerrville stays an optional cheap top-up.
- **West Texas:** the 109–118 mi legs exceed the safe leg even from 100%, and there are no extra CCS sites to add.
  - Drive 70 mph or less, and 65 mph or less on the Fort Stockton → Van Horn climb.
  - Leave Ozona, Fort Stockton and Van Horn at **100%**.
  - From Van Horn, head for bp pulse TA (106 mi) rather than EA Walmart (112 mi).
- **Add EA Deming:** Las Cruces → Deming (57 mi) → Lordsburg (61 mi) → Willcox (76 mi).
- **Add Rivian Gila Bend** ($0.67): Marana → Gila Bend (101 mi) → Dateland (52 mi) → Yuma (65 mi).
- **For the climb:** leave Yuma at 90% or more, and El Centro at **95–100%**.
- **Net effect:**
  - About 3 extra stops (Junction, Deming, Gila Bend) and roughly 2 h more charging and driving.
  - About $255–265 at non-member prices, or about $215–225 with Pass+.
