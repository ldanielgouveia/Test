# Cheapest EV Charging in San Antonio for a 2023 Hyundai Ioniq 5

_Researched October 3, 2026. Prices change often, so confirm in the network's app before you drive over._

## What the car can use

| | 2023 Ioniq 5 |
|---|---|
| Level 2 (AC) plug | J1772. The onboard charger takes up to **10.9 kW** (about 45–48 A). |
| DC fast plug | CCS1. The 800 V battery peaks around **230 kW or more** on 350 kW CCS chargers. 10→80% takes about 18–20 min. |
| Tesla Superchargers | Work with a NACS-to-CCS adapter. Hyundai gave a free adapter, through MyHyundai, to owners who bought or leased by Jan 31, 2025. Tesla's chargers run at a lower voltage, so the car tops out around **~100 kW** there. 10→80% takes about 35–45 min. |
| Tesla destination (Level 2) | Work with a Tesla-to-J1772 adapter. Almost always for hotel guests only. |

"10→80%" costs below assume the Long Range battery (77.4 kWh), which takes about **54 kWh**. The Standard Range battery (58 kWh) takes about 41 kWh.

**Speed:** public Level 2 units here are probably 6–7 kW, which adds about 20–25 miles of range per hour. The car can take 10.9 kW, but few public units supply that much.

**Data quality:** none of the "free" listings had a 2026 date. The dealership chargers are courtesy units and may favor customers. Check PlugShare or the ChargePoint app, or call, before relying on one.

### Other Level 2 options considered
- **City of San Antonio "EVSA" Blink chargers** (200+ ports at libraries, parks, the Zoo garage, St. Mary's St. garage). The Blink IQ 200 hardware can give the car its full 10.9 kW. Prices are set per site and none were confirmed (one Blink site was quoted at $0.25/kWh + $0.49/session).
- **Hotel Tesla destination chargers** (free for guests, adapter needed): Hyatt Regency Hill Country Resort, Grand Hyatt, Hyatt Regency Riverwalk, Comfort Suites (505 Live Oak St), Noble Inns – Oge Inn Riverwalk.
- **SAT airport garages:** the charging is free but garage parking fees apply.
- **IKEA Live Oak:** Blink Level 2 units (not free) plus a ChargePoint unit (price unknown).
- **The Pearl:** ChargePoint in a paid garage ($3–10 parking).

**Just missed: Circle K**, 5239 US Hwy 87 E, 78222. $0.43/kWh flat, 4 stalls, 180 kW, 24/7 (about $23 for 10→80%). It's the best cheap pick when you need real speed or more than one stall during the day.

**No free public DC fast chargers were found.** The dealership CCS units at Ingram Park Nissan and Gunn Nissan might be free but are unverified.

### Membership math
- **Tesla membership ($12.99/mo):** Converse drops to $0.18/kWh at night and $0.43 by day. At these prices it pays off above about 95 kWh a month of daytime Supercharging (about 2 stops), or about 215 kWh a month at night.
- **Electrify America Pass+ ($7/mo):** 25% off. Walmart Thousand Oaks goes from $0.56 to ~$0.42/kWh, with full 800 V speed. It pays off above about 50 kWh a month.
- **EVgo Plus ($6.99/mo, up to 15% off) / PlusMax ($12.99/mo, up to 30% off).**
- **Electrify America free sessions:** the 2022–23 Ioniq 5 came new with 2 years of free 30-min EA sessions. That has almost certainly expired unless the car was bought new after about Oct 2024.

### Coming soon

- **Electrify America at H-E-B #262**, 5602 Bandera Rd, Leon Valley 78238. Expected around Dec 2026.

## Sources and limitations

**Database.** The most complete source is the US Dept. of Energy's charger database (AFDC), which lists every public US station with its specs. This research environment's network policy blocked it (`developer.nlr.gov`, `afdc.energy.gov`) and also blocked Open Charge Map's API. Instead, the station list and specs come from **dcfctracker.com**, which mirrors the AFDC records (same station IDs) and adds tracked prices. They were cross-checked against Open Charge Map, ChargeHub, the Tesla, EVgo and Rivian Roamer station pages, CPS Energy, and Texas TDLR construction filings, all read through web-search summaries. Expect a few smaller dealership or independent sites to be missing; PlugShare counts ~190 DC fast chargers across the whole San Antonio–New Braunfels metro.
